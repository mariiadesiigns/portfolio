Brand Identity

A clear foundation for everything that followed.

We retained BeauTech’s logo and core colour palette while repositioning the brand from a conventional corporate business to a premium, trusted aviation leasing partner.

New typography, messaging and abstract imagery created a more sophisticated, forward-looking identity, balancing innovation with the credibility built over 15 years.

Website

A complete digital expression of the brand.

The website brings the identity, messaging, and business structure together in one system, from the first impression to detailed service and inventory pages.

It has to introduce the brand and organise real operational information at the same time. Clear hierarchy keeps the technical content readable; the visual system keeps every page unmistakably BeauTech.

Social Media

A system built for everyday communication.

Flexible layouts keep company news, events, and seasonal moments connected without making every post look the same.

Print & Merchandise

The brand, carried into the real world.

Stationery, event materials, travel accessories, and branded objects extend the identity beyond the screen—keeping every physical touchpoint consistent, useful, and unmistakably BeauTech.
