Dreamers is a Kyiv-based team working across investments, development, and hospitality — building projects that change how people stay, gather, and belong.

I designed the brand identity for Dreamers: a visual system that could hold several hospitality projects under one name, without flattening what makes each of them distinct.

The work covers the mark, colour, and graphic language, then carries that thinking into social, events, and physical applications.

Brand Identity

A clear foundation for everything that followed.

Dreamers needed one visual language for a group of hospitality projects — Ulis, Dream Hostel, Druzi, Reytarska Circle — that could still leave room for each place to feel like itself.

The system is built around a simple pin mark, a sky-and-coral palette, and a looping line that can move from a hiring post to an event screen without turning into a template.
